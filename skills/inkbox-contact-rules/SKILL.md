---
name: inkbox-contact-rules
description: Inspect channel-specific Inkbox contact rules.
---

Use `inkbox_list_mail_contact_rules`, `inkbox_list_phone_contact_rules`, or `inkbox_list_imessage_contact_rules` for the relevant channel. Report the effective rule without changing it. A policy on one channel does not imply permission on another. `inkbox_doctor` shows which optional tools are enabled.
